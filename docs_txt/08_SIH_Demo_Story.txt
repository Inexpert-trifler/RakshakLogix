RakshakLogix — SIH Demo & Presentation Story

Predict → Detect → Simulate → Optimize → Act

Opening Pitch

“Logistics failure is rarely caused by not having data. It is caused by discovering the problem too late. RakshakLogix turns fragmented logistics data into an early-warning and decision-support system.”

5-Minute Demo Flow

Open dashboard: readiness, inventory, alerts and map.

Open a forward location and show stock plus predicted runway.

Generate a 30-day demand forecast and show confidence/metrics.

Show a predicted stockout alert.

Create a replenishment shipment.

Run route/vehicle optimization and show ranked alternatives.

Launch ‘main route unavailable for 5 days’ simulation.

Show baseline versus scenario readiness and affected locations.

Show recommended mitigation plan.

Close with Predict → Detect → Simulate → Optimize → Act.

Judge Questions

Where is the AI? — Demand forecasting, risk prediction and constrained optimization.

What if data is incomplete? — Data-quality checks plus baseline/fallback models.

Why PostGIS? — Logistics is geographic and needs spatial queries.

Can real data be used? — Yes, through the normalized ingestion layer; MVP uses synthetic data.

Does AI act autonomously? — No, it provides explainable recommendations for human approval.

What is novel? — One integrated forward-looking loop across forecasting, inventory, routing and disruption simulation.

One-Line Pitch

RakshakLogix predicts supply needs, detects future shortages, optimizes movement, and simulates disruptions before they become logistics failures.
