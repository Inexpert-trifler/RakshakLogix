RakshakLogix — AI/ML & Optimization Specification

Forecasting, risk, routing and simulation

ML Philosophy

Use the simplest model that demonstrably improves the baseline. Store model version, training window, features, metrics and inference timestamp.

Demand Forecasting Pipeline

Validate and normalize historical consumption.

Create lag, rolling, calendar and seasonality features.

Join safe contextual features such as weather and location attributes.

Use chronological train/validation/test splits.

Compare against naive/seasonal-naive baseline.

Evaluate MAE, RMSE and MAPE.

Persist selected model version and metrics.

Generate forecast intervals where supported.

Candidate Models

Naive/seasonal naive — mandatory baseline.

Holt-Winters/exponential smoothing — strong simple baseline.

XGBoost — feature-rich prediction.

Statsmodels time-series models — structured temporal patterns.

Future: probabilistic forecasting after MVP validation.

Risk Engine

MVP uses an explainable composite score. Illustrative starting weights: inventory 30%, demand uncertainty 20%, weather 20%, route 15%, delivery 15%. Weights are configurable demo assumptions, not validated operational doctrine.

Optimization

Generate feasible route candidates.

Apply hard constraints: capacity, vehicle availability, source, destination and shipment requirements.

Score soft constraints: ETA, distance, reliability and risk.

Use OR-Tools where vehicle routing/allocation is appropriate.

Return ranked alternatives, not one unexplained answer.

What-if Simulation

Clone planning state in memory/scenario namespace.

Apply a disruption such as route unavailable, demand spike or severe weather.

Re-run inventory, route and risk calculations.

Compare baseline versus scenario.

Generate mitigation options with estimated impact.

Never mutate live operational state.

Explainability

Show major factors influencing each recommendation.

Show forecast confidence and model metrics.

Show constraints that ruled out alternatives.

Show trade-offs and expected impact.

Show model/algorithm version and timestamp.
