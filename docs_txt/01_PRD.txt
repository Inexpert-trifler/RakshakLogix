RakshakLogix — Product Requirements Document

Predictive Logistics & Forward Supply Chain | SIH PS 26251

Product Definition

RakshakLogix is an AI-assisted logistics decision-support platform that predicts supply demand, identifies future inventory shortages, evaluates route/transport risk, optimizes replenishment and simulates disruptions before they affect logistics operations.

Problem

Demand, inventory, transportation capacity, geography, terrain, weather and consumption data may exist in separate streams. The product creates a common operational picture and turns those streams into forward-looking recommendations.

Goals

Predict what supplies will be needed, where and over configurable horizons.

Predict inventory depletion and replenishment requirements before stockouts.

Score logistics risk using inventory, demand, weather, route and delivery factors.

Recommend feasible transportation and routing plans under constraints.

Simulate disruptions and compare baseline versus scenario outcomes.

Provide explainable recommendations with human approval.

Non-Goals

Not an autonomous command-and-control system.

Does not issue real-world military orders or control vehicles.

Does not use classified operational data in the hackathon MVP.

Does not provide weapon targeting or combat decision support.

Does not replace qualified logistics personnel.

Core User Journeys

Import consumption history → validate → forecast demand → review confidence.

Open location → inspect inventory runway → detect predicted stockout → receive replenishment recommendation.

Create shipment → evaluate vehicles/routes → optimize → review ranked options → approve.

Create disruption scenario → simulate → compare baseline vs scenario → inspect mitigation plan.

Open command dashboard → see readiness, critical alerts, affected locations and top recommendations.

Success Metrics

Forecast beats a simple baseline on holdout data.

High-risk synthetic stockouts are detected with useful lead time.

Optimization returns a feasible plan within demo time.

Simulation completes in seconds/low tens of seconds for the demo dataset.

Critical APIs have automated test coverage.

Recommendations expose their major contributing factors.

MVP Acceptance Criteria

Seed synthetic dataset loads end-to-end.

Location, item, inventory and consumption CRUD/import works.

Forecast is generated and persisted.

Inventory runway and risk are calculated.

Shipment and route optimization work.
